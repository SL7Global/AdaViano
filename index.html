WHY APRICOTS

A snack that carries
its own nutrition

Anatolian apricots are naturally dense in vitamins, fibre and energy — which is why they sell as a health snack as readily as a bakery ingredient. Every claim below is taken from the producer's own product descriptions.

Vitamins

Whole sundried and diced apricots alike are described as packed with vitamins — the reason they read as a wellness snack, not a confection.

Fibre

Naturally high in fibre, which is what makes dried apricot a staple of breakfast cereals, bars and trail mixes.

Natural energy

The sweetness is the fruit's own. Diced apricots are rich in natural energy, and the sundried whole fruit is offered as a delicious way to boost energy and well-being.

Healthy fats

Apricot kernels are nutrient-dense and rich in healthy fats, vitamins and minerals — a slightly nutty note for smoothies, salads and snacking.

THE PRODUCER AND THE DISTRIBUTOR

From the heart of Malatya, directly to your shelves.

The producer’s story began in 1982, trading dried apricots in the Darende district of Malatya — the region that grows most of the world’s dried apricots. By 1992 the next generation had taken the business over and begun exporting under its own name, and in 2010 it was established as a dried food and kernels company. It sells as the producer, not an intermediary.

AdaViano brings that portfolio to the United States. We hold one portfolio — nine products across apricots, organic preserves and olive oil — and sell it here: quotations, samples, certificates, orders and delivery.

Dried apricots are grown mainly in Malatya, which accounts for 85% of world production. Treated with sulphur they keep their bright orange colour; dried naturally they turn brown and take on a different texture.

marmalade cooked in Merzifon since

The preserve line goes back to rosehip marmalade cooked in traditional cauldrons over wood fire in the Merzifon district of Amasya. The same recipes are still small batch crafted from wild-harvested fruit gathered in its most natural state.

The business began in a small shop in the Darende district, trading dried apricots, and became a pioneer of that trade in the Turkish market. Exports under its own name followed in 1992, and the present company in 2010.
